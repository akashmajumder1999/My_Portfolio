special noodles recipe

make delicious noodles easily in just a few minutes.

required ingredients

  • noodles - 1 paket
  • egg - 1 pice
  • chopped onion and green chili - as needed
  • tomato sauce - 2 tablespoons

derections

  1. fast boil water in a pot and cook the noodles.
  2. heat oil in a pan and lightly fry the chopped onion and green chili.
  3. crack the egg into the pan abd scramble it.
  4. add the cooked noodles and seasoning stir well, mix in the tomato sauce, and serve.
